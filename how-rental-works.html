How Costume Rental Works

A simple, four-step process built around a minimum 3-day rental — from checking availability to returning the costume.

02Day 1 — CollectPick up the costume from the studio at House No 20, Seuj Nagar (West), Narengi Tinali, Guwahati.

Pricing, sizing & service area

💰 Pricing

Rental price is dynamic and depends on the specific costume — it starts from ₹300. Confirm the exact price for your chosen costume when you call or WhatsApp.

🔐 Security deposit

A refundable security deposit is required on every booking, in addition to the rental price. It's returned when the costume comes back on time and undamaged.

📅 Rental duration

Every rental is a minimum of 3 days: Day 1 pickup, Day 2 for your event, Day 3 return. Longer periods can be discussed for multi-day events.

📍 Service area

We serve customers across all of Guwahati. Costumes are collected and returned in person at our Narengi Tinali studio — home delivery isn't available at this time.

📏 Sizing & fit

Costumes and accessories are available for children roughly 1 to 14 years. A trial visit is the most reliable way to confirm fit; otherwise share age, height and weight with us.

🕐 Availability

We're reachable 24 hours a day by phone and WhatsApp for booking enquiries — call or message anytime to check a date.
