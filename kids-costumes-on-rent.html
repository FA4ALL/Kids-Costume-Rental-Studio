Before you book

Common questions

Can I try the costume on before booking?
Yes — a trial at the studio is the best way to confirm fit. If visiting isn't possible, share your child's measurements over phone or WhatsApp and we'll help you choose a size.
Are accessories included?
Many costumes include matching accessories (crowns, wands, masks, props); some accessories can be added separately depending on the costume. Confirm what's included when you book.
Can I buy a costume instead of renting?
Selected costumes and accessories are available for purchase alongside the rental range. Ask us which items are currently available to buy.
