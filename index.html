Need to know? Frequently Asked Questions View FAQs

How does the costume rental process work?
Choose your costume, check the dates and try it on for fit. Pay the booking amount and refundable security deposit. Collect it on Day 1, use it for your event on Day 2, and return it on Day 3. No washing is required before return.
For how many days can I rent a costume?
Our standard rental period is 2–3 days: pick up the day before your event and return it the day after. Longer rentals are available for school functions or multi-day events, depending on availability.
Do I need to wash or dry-clean the costume before returning it?
No. Please leave the cleaning to us. Costumes are professionally sanitized, steam-cleaned and packed hygienically after use.
Can I buy costumes directly instead of renting?
Yes. Most premium fancy-dress costumes are available for rent, while selected popular costumes and accessories are also available for direct purchase.
What age groups do you cater to?
We offer costumes and accessories for children from 1 year to 14 years, covering toddlers, preschoolers and school-aged children.
What happens if a costume doesn’t fit properly?
We recommend bringing your child for a quick trial at the studio. If that is not possible, share the child’s measurements and we’ll help you choose the best fit. Minor non-permanent adjustments may also be possible.
What is your security deposit policy?
A refundable security deposit is collected at pickup. If the costume is returned on time in its original condition without tears, damage or defects, the deposit is refunded as promised.
Do you provide matching accessories?
Yes! Crowns, magic wands, swords, masks, hats and themed props are included with many costumes or can be added separately, depending on the costume.
Do you offer customized return gifts or party items?
Yes. We are also expanding into personalized gifts, party favours and themed hampers for birthdays and special occasions. Contact us to discuss custom requirements.
