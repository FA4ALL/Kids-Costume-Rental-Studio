Need to know?

Quick answers

The questions we hear most from parents booking for the first time.

Where can I rent kids costumes in Guwahati?
At Kids Costume Rental Studio, House No 20, Seuj Nagar (West), Narengi Tinali, Guwahati. Call or WhatsApp +91 94019 46656 to check availability before you visit.
How much does it cost to rent a costume?
Rental pricing is dynamic and depends on the costume — it starts from ₹300. A refundable security deposit is mandatory on every booking. Confirm the exact price for your chosen costume by phone or WhatsApp.
How long can I keep the costume?
Every rental runs a minimum of 3 days: Day 1 pickup, Day 2 for your event, Day 3 return.
Do you deliver, or do I need to collect it myself?
Costumes are collected and returned at our Narengi Tinali studio. We serve customers across all of Guwahati, but home delivery isn't available at this time.
