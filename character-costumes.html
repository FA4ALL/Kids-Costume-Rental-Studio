Sizing & fit

Getting the right size

Character costumes are sized for children roughly 1 to 14 years, and fit varies by design. A quick trial at the studio is the most reliable way to confirm fit — if that's not possible, share your child's age, height and weight over phone or WhatsApp and we'll recommend the closest size.

Before you book

Common questions

Which superhero and cartoon characters are available?
Availability changes with stock — tell us the character your child wants and we'll confirm if it's in your size for your dates.
Can I book the same costume again for a future event?
Yes, subject to availability on your new date. Call or WhatsApp ahead to hold it.
What if the costume gets slightly dirty during the event?
Normal wear from a single event is expected — you don't need to wash or dry-clean it before returning. We handle professional cleaning after every rental.
